🥇 Template mais completo

Quick Question - Ingredient Supplier
Melhor porque não parece apenas uma checagem fria de contato. Ele explica quem somos, cita os ingredientes e faz uma pergunta simples sobre uso de fruit powders.

Template recomendado para escalar

Hi [Name], Nice to e-meet you! I'm Débora from New Açaí Amazonas — we’re a US-based manufacturer specializing in high-quality fruit powders and botanical extracts (Acai, Acerola, Blueberry, and more). We currently supply several dietary supplement brands, and I was curious — are you already using fruit powders in your formulations? If yes, I’d love to explore if we could support you with consistent supply, pricing, or new ingredient options. And if you're not the right person, would you mind pointing me to who handles ingredient sourcing or R&D? Appreciate it! 😊 Débora
